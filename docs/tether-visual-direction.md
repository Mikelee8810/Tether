# Tether visual direction — iOS glass motivation loop

## Product thesis

Tether is a private relationship game: it should make staying close feel warm, achievable, and rewarding, while quietly becoming a useful memory layer for each relationship.

## Representative dashboard hierarchy

1. **Your momentum:** level, Sparks, weekly consistency, and relationship pressure live in one compact progress surface.
2. **One meaningful action:** Today's Spark is the visual anchor and gives the user one obvious person to contact.
3. **Your people:** Inner Circle and Orbit Pulse make the network scannable without competing with the primary action.
4. **Supporting controls:** search, filters, settings, and tracking remain accessible but visually secondary.

## Visual language

- Use an iOS-inspired Liquid Glass functional layer: navigation, the progress surface, the primary hero, and sheets.
- Keep content underneath quieter. Repeated person rows may be translucent, but should not compete with hero surfaces through equal blur, elevation, or tint.
- Editorial serif is reserved for emotional headlines; Manrope handles names, numbers, controls, and utility copy.
- Cobalt means navigation and action. Flame means overdue/urgent. Amber means reward or due soon. Emerald means healthy. Violet supports level progression.
- Motion should reveal hierarchy and state: short staged entrances, progress fills, tactile press responses, and clear continuity when Today's Spark changes.
- Respect the system's reduced-motion setting; decorative motion must disappear when animations are disabled.

## Gamification rule

Scores and streaks exist to motivate real contact, not screen engagement. Every reward must map to a relationship action. Urgency remains visible, but the screen should offer one calm next step instead of repeating warnings everywhere.

## Anti-patterns

- Android/Material visual defaults as the product identity.
- Glass on every layer with identical elevation and tint.
- Several brightly tinted actions competing on one screen.
- Decorative AI sparkle language unrelated to a real relationship insight.
- Removing overdue pressure, Sparks, streaks, or level progress.
- Constant looping animation that makes a relationship app feel like a casino.

## Acceptance target for this phase

Only the main dashboard is the representative screen. It must read clearly on Pixel_Radar, preserve overdue motivation and gamification, feel recognizably iOS-glass-inspired, and remain calm enough for daily use before the direction expands to the rest of the app.
